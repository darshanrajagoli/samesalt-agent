How these numbers are made

  • One row per completed lookup: time, matched medicine, its price per unit, the cheapest same-salt brand and the Jan Aushadhi price per unit, language, the first 3 digits of the PIN code if typed, and an optional "did you switch?" answer.
  • No photos (they are read in memory and discarded), names, phone numbers, IP addresses or precise locations are stored.
  • Saving found = (scanned brand price − cheaper of lowest-priced same-salt brand and Jan Aushadhi) per unit × units in the scanned pack. It is the saving available on one pack, not money actually saved.
  • Reported saving counts only lookups where the person tapped "Yes, I switched".
  • Brand prices are printed MRPs from a November 2022 dataset; Jan Aushadhi prices are from the official PMBJP list (date above).
  • Test traffic (automated evals, demo recordings, developer checks) is flagged and excluded: — test lookups excluded so far.
  • Raw aggregate JSON: /api/impact · data status: /api/health
